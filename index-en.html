The practitioner

Forty-two years of a profession, then this work.

Born into a family living close to nature, and an accomplished sportsman, Luc first spent forty-two years in engineering and project management. He then turned to the knowledge passed down by his great-grandmother and his grandfathers.

He practises magnetism and bone-setting. That path also includes training as a druid healer, stated here as a fact, without ceremony. He does not promise a result. He accompanies.

How it unfolds

A session, in four steps.

01 Getting in touch

You call or write. A few minutes are enough to understand what you are going through and to choose a suitable session. No jargon, no pressure.

02 Preparing

Before the session, drink water and avoid coffee and alcohol if you can. Wear comfortable clothes. At a distance, choose a quiet place where you will not be disturbed.

03 The session

In person or remote, it lasts 30 to 60 minutes. Luc holds his hands above the area, speaks little, listens a lot. You stay dressed. You may close your eyes.

04 Afterwards

A pleasant tiredness is common. Drink, walk, sleep early. Luc remains reachable in the following days if something shifts or if you have a question.
